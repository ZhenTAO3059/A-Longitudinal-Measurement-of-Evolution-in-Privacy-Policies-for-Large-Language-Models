A cookie is mainly used for facilitating your use of our Services, and helping us count the number of independent visitors. With cookie technology, we can provide you with more thoughtful personalized services and allow you to set specific service options.

We use cookies and similar technologies to track the activities on our Services and store certain information. Tracking technologies used are beacons, tags, and scripts to collect and track information and to improve and analyze our services. The technologies we use may include:
2.1 Cookies or Browser Cookies One of the main purposes of cookies is to provide a time-saving practical function. For example, if you personalize the Services Platform, cookies will help you call your information in subsequent visits, simplifying the process of your personal data recording (such as one-click login). When you return to the main page of the Services Platform, terminology database can call the information you provided before, facilitating your use of your customized functions. Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.
